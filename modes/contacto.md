# Mode: contacto — LinkedIn Outreach

Generates a short, targeted LinkedIn message to a relevant contact at a company you've applied to or are about to apply to.

---

## Step 1 — Identify the right contact

Search LinkedIn for the company. Prioritize in this order:
1. **Hiring manager** for the specific team (best — they make the call)
2. **Recruiter** handling the role (good — they screen candidates)
3. **Peer** on the same team (useful for intel, not application advocacy)
4. **Data/analytics team member** at any level (for informational angle)

Use WebSearch: `site:linkedin.com/in "{company}" "data analyst" OR "analytics"` to find candidates.

---

## Step 2 — Classify the contact

| Type | Goal | Tone |
|------|------|------|
| Hiring Manager | Express fit, show you've done homework | Confident, specific |
| Recruiter | Flag application, offer to provide more context | Professional, concise |
| Peer | Learn about the team/role, genuine curiosity | Warm, no ask |
| Team member | Informational — what's the role really like? | Curious, no pressure |

---

## Step 3 — Generate message

**Hard constraints:**
- Under 300 characters (LinkedIn connection message limit)
- No phone number
- No "I'm passionate about" or "I would love to"
- No "just checking in" or "I noticed your profile"
- Must include a specific reason to respond

**Framework:**
1. **Hook** — one specific thing about their company or work (not generic)
2. **Proof point** — one concrete thing from cv.md that's relevant (with a metric)
3. **Soft ask** — light, easy to respond to (not "can you refer me?")

**Templates by contact type:**

**Hiring Manager:**
```
Hi [Name] — saw [Company] is hiring a [role]. My CSV Analyst Bot cut analysis time 80% using Python/Streamlit. Would love 10 min to hear what the team is working on.
```

**Recruiter:**
```
Hi [Name] — applied for the [role] at [Company] yesterday. Happy to share more context on my data projects if useful. [Aidan Kuo]
```

**Peer:**
```
Hi [Name] — noticed you're on the [team] at [Company]. I'm applying for the [role] and curious what data tooling the team uses day-to-day. Any insight appreciated.
```

---

## Step 4 — Output

Provide 2 message options (one shorter, one fuller) ready to copy-paste into LinkedIn.

Note: If the contact is a recruiter, also offer to draft a follow-up email for after they respond.
